Core features

Built for a practical MVP that can be tested safely inside a pharmacy workflow.

Prescription OCR

Upload a prescription image, fax, or scan and convert visible text into editable structured prescription fields.

AI field extraction

Identify likely drug name, strength, sig, quantity, refills, prescriber, and patient details in a clean review layout.

Confidence scoring

Show low-confidence sections so pharmacy staff can quickly focus on handwriting, missing details, or unclear directions.

Pharmacist verification

Require human review before anything is saved, exported, copied, or entered into a pharmacy management system.

Risk flags

Flag missing prescriber information, ambiguous strengths, controlled-drug review needs, and look-alike/sound-alike concerns.

Audit trail

Track who uploaded, reviewed, edited, approved, exported, or deleted prescription intake records.

How it works

Upload prescription

Staff upload a scanned prescription, fax image, phone image, or PDF.

OCR reads the document

The system extracts visible text and separates it into possible prescription fields.

AI structures the data

The extracted text is converted into a pharmacist-friendly review screen.

Unclear items are flagged

Low-confidence handwriting, missing details, and unusual combinations are highlighted.

Pharmacist verifies

The pharmacist or authorized pharmacy team member reviews against the original image before use.

Export or copy forward

Verified data can be copied or exported to support prescription entry workflows.

Designed with pharmacy safety in mind

RxVision AI should be positioned as an intake support tool, not an autonomous prescribing, dispensing, or clinical decision-making tool.
